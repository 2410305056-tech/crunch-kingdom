## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, architectural minimalism engineered for contemporary Indian culinary commerce and artisanal crunch experiences. The interface projects high-precision luxury, vibrant kinetic energy, and supreme clarity. Designed for discerning food lovers, tastemakers, and modern epicures, the visual language balances intense atmospheric depth with focused moments of warmth.

The aesthetic philosophy unites minimalist restraint with targeted energetic luminescence:
- **Atmospheric Lit-Dark Surfaces:** Rich obsidian depths serve as a vast, non-distracting architectural canvas.
- **Architectural Typography:** Uncompromising, hyper-clean letterforms deliver immediate legibility and editorial rhythm.
- **Electric Golden Core:** Restrained, high-impact hits of golden saffron convey crispness, crunch, freshness, and action without sensory clutter.
- **Negative Space as Luxury:** Generous voids, deliberate spacing increments, and hairline dividers create an uncluttered, premium gallery-like atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette adheres strictly to a 60-30-10 composition ratio to preserve visual calm and extreme functional hierarchy:

- **60% Obsidian Canvas (Dominant Surfaces):**
  - Base Ground: `#0F1117` (Deepest canvas layer).
  - Raised Surface: `#161922` (Card backings, sheets, and navigational shelves).
  - Elevated Accent Surface: `#1C202D` (Floating panels, hover states, and input tracks).
- **30% Porcelain Structure (Text, Dividers, & Structural Wireframes):**
  - Primary Typography: `#FFFFFF` (High contrast, crisp titles and primary data points).
  - Secondary Typography & Subtle Metadata: `#F1F3F9` (At 80% and 65% opacity).
  - Structural Wire Boundaries: `#282D3C` (Ultra-fine 1px perimeter outlines, dividers, and framing lines).
- **10% Saffron Core (Energy & High-Impact Directives):**
  - Active Energy Accent: `#FFB800` (Used exclusively for primary calls-to-action, status indicators, active selection tabs, and critical metric highlights). Never use this color for large background fields or secondary blocks.

## Typography

The type scale relies entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver an authoritative yet contemporary voice. Precision tracking and generous line heights ensure that short-form copy retains maximum breathing room against dark backdrops.

- **Headlines & Display:** Heavy weights (700 to 800) with slight negative tracking create impactful, architectural statements that anchor sections.
- **Body:** Kept at standard regular weight (400) in porcelain tones (`#F1F3F9`) with ample vertical leading to eliminate eye strain.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or high-tracking small sizes (10px–12px) provide systematic, crisp orientation tags across interfaces.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is built on an intentional fluid-grid system bound by strict structural margins. Content must never crowd the viewport boundaries.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, dynamic gutter of `1.5rem` (`24px`), outer safety margins of `3rem` (`48px`). Max container constraints cap at `1280px` to maintain strict focus and intentional edge space.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, gutter of `1.25rem` (`20px`), margins of `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout, gutter of `1rem` (`16px`), outer margins of `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Component stacks adhere strictly to multiples of 8 (`space-sm`, `space-md`, `space-lg`, `space-xl`, `space-2xl`). Generous vertical clearance (`space-2xl`) must separate distinct structural modules to reinforce the open, gallery-inspired aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, messy drop shadows. Instead, it relies on tonal stacking paired with delicate, high-precision boundaries:

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#0F1117`. Baseline surface containing zero border and zero blur.
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Containers):** `#161922` framed by a 1px solid stroke of `#282D3C`. Creates crisp delineation without visual weight.
- **Level 2 (Popovers, Flyouts, & Menus):** `#1C202D` with a 1px solid outline of `#282D3C` supplemented by an ultra-subtle ambient drop shadow (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` at 24px blur, 0px offset).
- **Saffron Luminescence:** Critical active elements and key CTAs project an ambient, low-spread saffron glow (`rgba(255, 184, 0, 0.15)` at 16px blur) to simulate warmth against the dark obsidian void.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is crisp, structured, and disciplined. Soft radius treatments (`roundedness: 1`) provide just enough curvature to feel modern and human while keeping UI perimeters razor-sharp and architectural.

- **Micro Components (Chips, Inputs, Buttons):** 4px border radius (`0.25rem`).
- **Containers & Surface Cards:** 8px border radius (`0.5rem`).
- **Modal Dialogs & Floating Drawers:** 12px border radius (`0.75rem`).
- Pill shapes are prohibited except for miniature round status indicators (tags under 20px height).

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Action:* Solid `#FFB800` background with deep obsidian text (`#0F1117`), bold weight, 4px corner radius. On hover, shifts to an intense warm glow with zero layout shift.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Transparent background enclosed by a 1px `#282D3C` border, text in `#FFFFFF`. On hover, the surface shifts to `#161922` with a border transition to `#F1F3F9` at 40% opacity.
- **Inputs & Form Controls:**
  - Input field backings sit on `#161922` with 1px `#282D3C` perimeter strokes. Placeholder text sits at 40% opacity `#F1F3F9`. Focused fields gain a clean 1px `#FFB800` outline with no loud glow rings.
- **Cards:**
  - Built from `#161922` with a 1px `#282D3C` border and 8px border radius. Padding is uniformly generous (`space-lg` to `space-xl`). Card media sits flush or with uniform negative margins.
- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Compact geometry using 4px radius. Inactive badges use `#1C202D` fill with `#F1F3F9` text. Active or accent chips use `#FFB800` text alongside a 10% opacity `#FFB800` background fill with a hairline border.
- **Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios):**
  - Unselected states use 1px `#282D3C` strokes on `#161922`. Selected states fill with `#FFB800` and display obsidian checkmarks for pristine contrast.
- **Lists & Data Dividers:**
  - Minimal list items separated strictly by 1px hairline horizontal rules in `#282D3C`. Generous vertical padding (`space-md`) ensures maximum readability and effortless skimming.
